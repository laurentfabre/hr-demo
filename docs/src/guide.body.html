The HR demo: every detail, and how to present it

A bilingual (FR/EN) Databricks App for HR leaders. It covers governed self-service, workforce capacity decisions, group reconciliation after an acquisition, Genie agents and an audited, human-reviewed action loop. All data is synthetic, and you can deploy it to your own workspace in about 30 minutes.

01 — Overview

One app, six experiences, one honest story

The core message: “An HR question, a useful answer, and only the data you are authorized to see.” Every experience shows the same pattern: governed data, then an explainable answer, then a human-reviewed decision with an audit trail. Pick the entry point that matches your buyer.

Golden rule

02 — Architecture

What actually runs, and what is simulated

Everything you click is executed for real: SQL, Lakebase transactions, model calls, Genie and the audit trail. Only two inputs are simulated, and both are labeled in the app: the GCP source export and the Dust-style interface.

ConcernHow the demo handles it
IdentityDatabricks Apps authenticates the caller. A deployment-owned binding maps the presenter's IdP email to their workspace user ID, and unmatched identities are denied. There is no email-domain wildcard.
Scopeenterprise.access assigns entities, a reader/editor role and a tenant per identity. Queries run as the App service principal with server-side entity filtering. This is not Genie OBO or database row-level security.
ActionsPropose → explicit review (expires after 15 min) → commit to the synthetic queue → read back. Nothing is ever written to Workday, GCP, Dust or another HR system.
Source ingestionPayload hash binding, contract validation and a transactional upsert create a new revision. Bad batches are recorded without changing accepted rows, and a new revision invalidates uncommitted approvals.
EvidenceThe Technical evidence tab shows the executed SQL and parameters, revision hash, lineage, p50/p95 latency, tokens and the identity audit.
Tool contractGET /api/enterprise/tools/manifest and POST /api/enterprise/tools/query form a read-only contract with no arbitrary SQL and no write path. OpenAPI is published at /api/openapi.json.

04 — Enterprise & Global (default)

Three buyer stories on one connected workflow

All three stories share the same synthetic dataset and API. Before you click anything, choose the story that matches the person in the room.

StoryBuyer & triggerDecisionMeasurable evaluation
Fund the capacity that protects deliveryCFO, CHRO, COO. Committed delivery exceeds capacity.Hire vs redeploy budget trade-offReconciled gap, agreed cost/effect assumptions, reviewed action readback
Reconcile the group after an acquisitionCHRO, CFO, data platform. An acquisition or reorganization.Common group definitions with local continuityEmployee/contractor bridge, FX, fiscal calendar, effective-dated rollup
Answer in the employee's existing workspaceCIO, CHRO, security. HR depends on data specialists.Governed tools in the existing agent interfaceTwo authenticated scopes, cited policy, source availability, latency, maintenance effort

The 5-minute executive path

  1. 0:00–0:45Confirm the decision. Ask for the buyer's decision and one measurable baseline. Then click New rehearsal, which preserves all prior history.
  2. 0:45–1:30Scope. Select France and 2026-09. Point at the capacity gap (FTE-months) and the “Revision 1 · lakebase” badge.
  3. 1:30–2:30Change the data. Click Ingest France demand increase. The revision increments and the FR gap moves from 43 to 73 FTE-months. For the Group story, open Group & sources and show the definition bridge instead.
  4. 2:30–3:45Explain and decide. Click Ask the data for a live, revision-grounded answer. Then fill the capacity proposal and its rationale, review it explicitly, commit it, and show the readback.
  5. 3:45–5:00Value. On Value & costs, compare four alternatives and show the low/high range. Staff time is never counted as cash. Agree the next decision.
10 and 20 minutes

10 min: also show source rejection and recovery on Group & sources (upload a bad batch, then Recover baseline source) and an external-tool read on /agent-workspace. 20 min: also show the HQ vs local identity proof (FR reader cannot read DE or write), the Technical evidence tab, the day-two ownership table and an evaluation plan you fill in together on the Account brief tab.

05 — Historical four-domain cockpit

The 20-minute HR leadership flow

This is the original People Ops / HR storyline. Personas switch in this order: Claire (CPO) → Sofia (HRBP France) → Mehdi (TA lead) → Claire → Alex (People Analytics). It opens with a useful aggregate answer, then shows a denied, audited unauthorized request.

2–4 min/trustInspect the real session, switch the persona preview, request an individual-risk detail and show the denial in the audit.

UI limits: proposal rationale 10–600 characters, review notes 4–600. Approvals expire after 15 minutes, after which you need a fresh proposal and review. Rehearsal notes must say AUTOMATED TEST - no actual human approval when automated.

Each Genie space sits on one UC Metric View over a 216-row synthetic cohort table (scenario × region × department × month, Jan–Jun 2026).

Smoking gun

The Engineering / EMEA hotspot begins in April. In the baseline scenario, the Supervisor calls all four tools and returns a 30 FTE gap, 66 days mean fill, 2.79% monthly attrition and 13 overdue cases. Present it as an association, not a cause. Genie answers come with the actual SQL, rows and statement/conversation IDs. The Supervisor exposes the specialists' returned tables.

07 — Demo Studio

Tailor the story before the meeting

On My studio / Mon studio, pick a fictional industry and name, reorder the playbooks and choose a 5, 10 or 20-minute format. Present my story opens a five-beat presentation (Escape exits). The capacity lab lets you edit retain/hire/redeploy counts, unit costs, effects and budget. A simulation is an option for discussion and never an approval. Export to JSON to reuse a configuration, or to a Markdown presenter brief.

Always
  • Say “synthetic” early. The banner stays visible on every page.
  • Keep retention signals at team/cohort level.
  • Label modeled results as modeled: ROI, FX and capacity effects are assumptions.
  • Say live vs recorded explicitly when you switch to a fallback.
  • Remind the room that a human approves, and that the approval expires.
Never
  • Claim that the persona picker grants access.
  • Show individual flight-risk scores, candidate rankings or personal salary comparisons.
  • Call it a Dust or GCP integration. Both are simulated; only the HTTP contract is real.
  • Present a vendor name (Workday, Lucca, Greenhouse…) as the customer's actual stack.
  • Treat country filters as proof of data residency or legal compliance.

Discovery questions

What business event makes this urgent? · Which data is missing, versus already available? · Which source or metric definitions disagree?

Which interface must remain (HRIS, Dust, Slack…)? · What is the current cost and maintenance effort?

Who owns the budget, metric, source, access and run? · What result would justify moving forward, and by when?

09 — Objections

Evidence-based answers

ConcernResponse
Our HR suite already reports thisKeep the suite when one source and standard definitions are enough. Demonstrate the incremental cross-system or operational requirement before adding a platform.
We already have BICompare on the same dataset and metric. Test missing source data, shared definitions, permissions and action readback, not aesthetics.
We built an MCPKeep the working tool if it meets the need. Measure maintenance, cross-source semantics, rights and cost on the same bounded case.
We use DustKeep the chosen interface. The demo simulates Dust; the authenticated HTTP tool contract is real. Validate actual tenant compatibility before claiming an integration.
What about run effort?Walk through one new table, a failed batch, a metric change and an access update. Name the owner and record the actual minutes. Do not promise zero engineering.
Is the ROI real?All demo benefits are modeled. Replace the estimates with the customer's baseline, show low/high realization and delay, and separate staff capacity from cash.
Is it globally compliant?Show the actual execution location and assigned access. Country filters and synthetic policies do not prove residency or employment-law compliance.

10 — Deploy it in your workspace

From clone to live demo

The repo ships the prebuilt wheel and frontend, so deployment needs no Node, no APX and no access to the authoring repository. Use an FEVM or another non-production workspace with a dedicated Lakebase database. Never use a production HR database.

Prerequisites

  • Python 3.11+, uv, Databricks CLI ≥ 1.19, and an explicitly selected, authenticated profile (databricks auth login --profile <p>)
  • A Lakebase project, branch, endpoint and dedicated database (for example hr_demo)
  • A READY chat endpoint on FMAPI for the live explanation, for example databricks-claude-opus-4-7
  • Optional: a SQL warehouse, Genie spaces and a Supervisor for /ask, /dnb and live briefings (see below)

Steps

# 1. Install
git clone <this-repo> hr-demo && cd hr-demo
uv sync --project app

# 2. Target config (never commit it: *.local.json is gitignored, never store a token)
cp target.example.json target.local.json
#   profile, app_name (lowercase, <=26 chars), presenter_user_id + presenter_user_name
#   (from: databricks current-user me --profile <p>), lakebase_resource, lakebase_connection,
#   enterprise.model_endpoint. PGUSER is filled in by "provision".

# 3. Lifecycle (use a NEW --out file for each run: prior evidence is never overwritten)
PY=app/.venv/bin/python; P=<profile>; C=target.local.json
$PY delivery/manage.py preflight --profile $P --config $C --out runs/01-preflight.json
$PY delivery/manage.py provision --profile $P --config $C --out runs/02-provision.json
$PY delivery/manage.py deploy    --profile $P --config $C --out runs/03-deploy.json
$PY delivery/manage.py verify    --profile $P --config $C --out runs/04-verify.json
CommandWhat it does
preflightChecks that the authenticated user matches the configured presenter, that the Lakebase endpoint exists and that the model endpoint is READY.
provisionCreates the App if it is absent, then binds the Lakebase database (CAN_CONNECT_AND_CREATE) and the model (CAN_QUERY) and writes the App service principal into PGUSER. It never creates or deletes a Lakebase project.
deployRenders app.yml env from the config, uploads the wheel, requirements and config to your workspace folder, and deploys the App.
verifyCalls /api/enterprise/catalog as you and expects 3 stories and the configured scope.
resetCreates a new rehearsal run through the API. Previous runs, approvals and audit are kept.
stop / teardown --confirm-app <name>Stops the App's compute (data kept), or deletes only an App that this workflow created. Shared databases, warehouses and models are never touched.

Provisioning scripts

platform/dnb_setup.py (metric views, 4 Genie spaces, Supervisor) and platform/genie_setup.py (historical aggregate Genie + Supervisor) are the original authoring scripts. They are hardcoded to the author's FEVM profile, catalog/schema and repo layout, so read platform/README.md and adapt them before running. Grant the App service principal CAN_RUN on each Genie space, CAN_QUERY on the Supervisor endpoints and SELECT on the tables.

More identities (HQ vs local proof)

Map extra users in presenter_bindings (IdP email → workspace ID) and give them a scope in enterprise.access, keyed by workspace user ID. For example: {"<id>": {"entities": ["FR"], "role": "reader", "tenant": "aster"}}. Entities are DE, FR, GB, IN, JP and US. The presenter defaults to editor on all entities. Sign in as the FR reader to show that DE is denied and that writes are refused.

Rebuild from source (optional)

Install Node 24+ and APX, then run npm install && apx build in app/ and run deploy again. Lockfiles are deliberately excluded, so dependencies resolve from your registry.

11 — Before, during and after

Rehearse, reset, fall back

T-30 min

Pre-flight

Start the App (FEVM Apps compute may be stopped), run verify, open /enterprise and click New rehearsal. Ask one Genie question to warm it up (the first answer can take about a minute). Use a desktop viewport and pick the language.

During

If something breaks

If the model is unavailable, the SQL stays inspectable and the failure is explicit, so retry. If the session is lost, switch to the recorded walkthrough and say: “I'm switching to a prerecorded capture of the synthetic demo.” Never present playback as live.

After

Reset & cost

manage.py reset creates a fresh run for the next demo, and manage.py stop stops App compute. Keep Lakebase autoscaling small (the reference instance uses 0.5–1 CU). Delete the App with teardown when you are done.

Recorded mode · 2026-10-08
Enterprise walkthrough (automated run)
Fallback recording from a real deployed run. Its approvals are automated tests, not human approvals.
12 — Limits & repo map

What this demo does not prove

Proven by execution
  • Clean install, tests and the full preflight → teardown lifecycle from the packaged runtime wheel, in an isolated workspace App/database
  • HQ vs FR scoped identities, denial audit, rejected-batch recovery
  • Bounded load test: 7,200 rows, concurrency 4, HTTP p50 295 ms / p95 312 ms
  • All 20 DNB benchmarks match the reference SQL. The Supervisor uses all 4 tools.
Not proven / out of scope
  • Any customer's data, HRIS, Dust tenant or GCP connection
  • Data residency, legal compliance, or source-permission propagation
  • Measured ROI. All value figures are modeled.
  • Full mobile usability. Arbitrary Genie wordings are answered correctly only probabilistically.
